Walk it yourself

The 3D walkthrough opens this scheme in self-guided mode: move with the arrow keys or the on-screen pad, look around by dragging, switch day and night, and jump between rooms. It is the real-time model the photographs were made from, so it reads like a game rather than a photo.

What these pictures are. The renovation does not exist yet, so none of this is a photograph of a real room. Every image comes from one 3D model of the proposed ground floor (drawing A12-001): the light is simulated physically, then an image model turns the simulation into a camera-like photograph while keeping layout, furniture, colours and camera angle. Fine details such as fabric patterns, decor and the view through the windows are the model's plausible interpretation, not a specification.

Twelve schemes: three styles (Traditional, Mediterranean, Modern-Mediterranean hybrid) in four variations each (Vintage, Classic, Transitional, Modern). Room sizes, openings and furniture positions follow the drawing; ceiling height (3.0 m), door and window heights, lighting and all furnishing are assumptions.
