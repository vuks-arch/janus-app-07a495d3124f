Our Story

A saloon built on steady hands and good company.

Four generations ago, Vincenzo opened a two-chair shop on Oak Street. We've kept the brass fixtures, the leather chairs and the same simple promise: every client leaves looking their best.

  • ✓
    Master barbers only

    Each stylist trains for three years before taking a chair of their own.

  • ✓
    Traditional hot-towel shaves

    Straight razor, badger brush and a lather made fresh every morning.

  • ✓
    Small-batch products

    Locally blended oils, balms and tonics — never anything we wouldn't use ourselves.
